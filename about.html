About us

We help seniors in northern Utah live on their own terms.

Strickler LLC is a northern Utah company. Our goal is to help seniors stay independent while giving them and their families a device for safety.

We set up devices from Becklar, whose monitoring centers answer every call. You get a local contact who helps you choose, set up and use your device.
