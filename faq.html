Questions & FAQs

Getting started

What is a Belle medical alert device?

Belle devices are mobile personal emergency response systems (mPERS) designed to help people get assistance during an emergency. With the press of a button, the wearer can connect directly to a 24/7 emergency monitoring operator.

Do I need a landline or home phone?

No. Belle devices use built-in cellular technology. You do not need a landline or personal cell phone to operate the device.

Can I use my Belle device outside my home?

Yes. Belle devices are designed for mobile use, allowing you to take them shopping, walking, traveling, or visiting family. Cellular coverage is required.

Does the device contact 911 directly?

The device connects to a professional emergency monitoring operator. The operator can assess the situation and coordinate appropriate assistance, including emergency services when needed.

Calling for help

How do I call for help?

Simply press and hold the emergency button until the device activates. It will connect you to a monitoring operator through its built-in speaker and microphone.

What happens after I press the emergency button?

Your device initiates a connection with the monitoring center. An operator will communicate with you, assess the situation, and help coordinate the appropriate response.

Can I talk directly to the emergency operator?

Yes. Both Belle W and Belle X have built-in two-way voice communication, allowing you to speak directly with the monitoring operator.

What if I cannot speak after pressing the button?

The monitoring center will still receive the alert and can use available information to help determine the appropriate response. You do not have to be able to explain the emergency for the alert to be initiated.

Fall detection

What happens if I fall and cannot press the button?

When fall detection is enabled, the device can recognize certain movements associated with a fall and automatically initiate an emergency call.

Does fall detection work 100% of the time?

No. Automatic fall detection is an additional safety feature, not a guarantee. Some falls may not be detected, and certain movements may trigger false alarms. Always press the emergency button if you need help and are able to do so.

Can I cancel a false fall-detection alarm?

Yes. If the device detects a possible fall accidentally, you may have an opportunity to cancel the alert before it proceeds. Follow the device’s voice prompts.

Belle W (wrist)

How long does the Belle W battery last?

Battery life can last up to approximately 7 days per charge, depending on settings and usage. Some configurations may require more frequent charging.

Can I wear the Belle W in the shower?

The device is IP67 water-resistant. It is designed to withstand exposure to water, but it should not be submerged or intentionally taken swimming.

Is the Belle W comfortable to wear all day?

Yes. It is lightweight and designed for everyday wear. Its wristband design makes it convenient for individuals who prefer not to wear a pendant.

Belle X (lanyard or belt clip)

What makes the Belle X different from the Belle W?

The Belle X is designed primarily as a pendant worn around the neck or with an appropriate clip. The Belle W is designed as a wrist-worn device. Both provide access to emergency assistance.

Does the Belle X include automatic fall detection?

Yes. The Belle X supports automatic fall detection. This feature can initiate an emergency call when a qualifying fall is detected.

Can I wear the Belle X while sleeping?

Yes. Wearing the device consistently, including while sleeping, helps ensure that emergency assistance is available when needed. Remember to charge it when necessary.

Does the Belle X have GPS tracking?

The Belle X uses location technologies to help monitoring personnel identify your location during an emergency. Available location information depends on cellular coverage and environmental conditions.

Charging and care

How do I charge my Belle device?

Place the device into its designated charging cradle and connect the charger to a power outlet. Make sure it is properly seated and charging.

What happens if my battery dies?

If the battery is depleted, the device cannot provide its normal emergency alert functionality until it is recharged. Keep the device charged and respond to low-battery notifications.

Do I need to test my device?

Yes. It is a good practice to test your device regularly, ideally once a month, following the testing instructions provided by your service provider.

What happens if my device stops working?

Contact Strickler LLC for assistance with troubleshooting, service questions, or replacement options.

Coverage and setup

Does the Belle device work without Wi-Fi?

Yes. Belle devices use cellular connectivity for communication with the monitoring center. Wi-Fi may also be used as part of the device’s location technology.

Will the device work while traveling?

Yes, provided the device has access to a supported cellular network. It is a mobile device designed to travel with you.

Will the device work inside my home?

In most locations with adequate cellular coverage, yes. It is still important to test the device in the areas where you spend the most time.

Is the device ready to use after purchasing?

The device must be properly activated and associated with an active monitoring subscription before it can provide emergency monitoring services. Follow the activation instructions provided by Strickler LLC.
